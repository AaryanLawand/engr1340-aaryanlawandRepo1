Multiplication between two integers takes the two factors(integers) and creates a number of groups of 1 of the factors, the number of groups is determined by the other factor. These groups are summed up and results in the product(final value of multiplication).
4 * 6 = 24, 4 and 6 are both factors, 24 is the product. 4 groups of 6 add up to 24 and vis versa.
